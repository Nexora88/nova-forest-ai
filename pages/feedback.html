NEXORA / BUILT WITH FEEDBACK

Help make environmental data more useful.

NexoraWildfire AI is being developed around practical needs: understanding local environmental conditions, keeping track of selected areas, and making available observations easier to explore. Tell us what works, what breaks, and what would genuinely help in the field.

Report a bug or suggest a feature → Explore the product

What feedback is most useful?

  • A reproducible bug: what page you opened, what you expected, and what happened.
  • A data issue: the selected area, the layer, the time, and whether the source showed an error or no observations.
  • A practical feature request: who it helps and what task it would make easier.
  • Mobile usability: device/browser type and the control that was difficult to use.

Please do not include passwords, API keys, private contact details, or sensitive exact locations in a public issue. Public reports are visible to other visitors.
